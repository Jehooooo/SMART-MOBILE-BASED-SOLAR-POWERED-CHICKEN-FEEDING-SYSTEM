----------------------------------------------------------------------
C. CONCERNS AND LIMITATIONS TO DISCUSS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Connectivity
44. Is there reliable Wi-Fi at the test site? Farms are often outside
    router range. Is a mobile hotspot / pocket Wi-Fi acceptable?
45. If Wi-Fi is lost, the feeder will still feed using the RTC schedule,
    but alerts cannot be sent until it reconnects. Is this acceptable and
    should it be stated as a limitation?

Power
46. How many days should the system run without sunlight (rainy season)?
    This decides the battery size.
47. The ESP32 with Wi-Fi uses roughly 80-240 mA continuously. Is a deep
    sleep / power-saving mode required, or is constant connection preferred
    for real-time alerts?
48. Is the system protected against reverse polarity, short circuit and
    overcharge (fuse, charge controller settings)?

Dispensing Accuracy
49. The load cell sits under the dispensing outlet / tray. If chickens eat
    from the same tray, the weight reading will change while dispensing.
    Should the weighing tray be separate from the eating trough?
50. Feed falling onto the scale causes impact spikes - an overshoot of a
    few grams is expected. Is a small tolerance acceptable?
51. Humidity and moisture can make feed clump and jam the dispenser. How
    will the hopper be protected from rain and moisture?

Environment & Durability
52. Will the device be placed indoors (under a roof) or outdoors? Is a
    weatherproof enclosure required for the electronics?
53. The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor is not waterproof and can be affected by
    dust. Can a waterproof JSN-SR04T be used instead?
54. How will the electronics be protected from chickens pecking at wires
    and from rodents?
55. Will dust from the feed affect the ultrasonic readings over time?

Scope Limitations (to state in the paper)
56. The system does not monitor water supply, temperature, or chicken
    health - should these be listed as delimitations?
57. One feeder unit only - multiple-feeder management is out of scope?
58. Feed type limited to one type (e.g., pellets) for testing?
59. Results may only apply to the specific test site and flock size.

Budget & Timeline
60. What is the total budget for the prototype? Who shoulders the cost of
    the Firebase / cloud service (free tier should be enough)?
61. What is the deadline for the working prototype, the testing period, and
    the final defense?
62. Who on the team is responsible for: hardware assembly, firmware,
    mobile app, data gathering, and documentation?

Document Corrections Noticed

64. The Materials and Equipment section does not list the load cell,
    HX711, ultrasonic sensor, DC-DC step-down regulator, and power switch,
    although they appear in the Parts and Functions table. Should the list
    be updated for consistency?
65. The User Acceptability list in the Research Design paragraph is missing
